# 29. Important Content Rule

Do not publish unverified claims such as:

-   Number of clients
-   Number of completed projects
-   Years of experience
-   Certifications
-   Awards
-   International offices
-   Specific technology partnerships
-   Guaranteed results

unless IntelliSoft Innovations has officially confirmed them.

The website should prioritize **truth, clarity, strong portfolio
evidence, and real client outcomes** over inflated marketing claims.
